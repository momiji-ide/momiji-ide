🍁 LANDING PAGE IMPROVEMENT GUIDE

Momiji IDE

Actionable improvements to increase conversion, trust, and viral growth — based on current landing page analysis.

1. Hero Section

HIGH IMPACT
Add a hero demo video / GIF (30–60 sec)

This is the single highest-ROI improvement. Visitors can't feel the product without seeing it. A 30-second Loom or GIF showing Kitsune writing a React component and then explaining it in Block mode would 3–5× increase time-on-page.

What to show in the video:

  • User types a prompt in natural language → Kitsune writes code in real time
  • Switch to Block view to show the same logic visually
  • Paste a screenshot error → Kitsune reads and fixes it (Vision mode)
  • End on download CTA with a snappy voice-over
HIGH IMPACT
Refine the headline to lead with the user outcome
CURRENT
"Code smarter with Kitsune AI your on-call genius"
SUGGESTED
"Build anything — even if you've never coded before.
Kitsune AI does the heavy lifting."

The current headline speaks to developers. The new one captures beginners and creative professionals — your actual differentiator.

MEDIUM IMPACT
Add social proof numbers below the CTA
⬇ 2,400+ downloads  ·  ★ 480 GitHub stars  ·  🌍 38 countries

Even small real numbers build trust. Once you have them, put them right below the download button. Update weekly.

2. BYOK / AI Model Clarity

HIGH IMPACT
Explain the "bring your own key" model upfront

Many visitors will wonder: "If it's free, what's the catch with AI?" Answer it proactively — it builds trust and removes the friction of downloading and discovering this later.

ADD NEAR THE AI PROVIDERS SECTION
🔑 Your keys, your cost, your privacy.
Momiji connects to AI providers using your own API keys.
Keys are stored on your machine only — never sent to our servers.
Groq is available free with no key required to get started.

3. Target Audience Clarity

HIGH IMPACT
Add a "Who is Momiji for?" section

Currently the page feels developer-focused despite the product targeting non-coders too. Explicitly calling out audience segments creates instant recognition for the right people.

SUGGESTED SECTION
🧒 Kids & Students — Learn to code without the frustration. Start with blocks, grow into code.

🎮 Game Developers — Visual flow logic + AI agent for Unity/Godot scripting. No context switching.

🎨 Designers & Animators — Automate your workflow with visual nodes. No syntax required.

⚡ Developers — Full Monaco + Kitsune AI + 7 agents. Your power IDE, supercharged.

🏢 Teams & Studios — Share memory, collaborate, customize Kitsune for your stack.

4. Kitsune AI Section

MEDIUM IMPACT
Show Kitsune's personality more — it's a differentiator

The current character profile section is great but buried. Consider pulling a Kitsune quote to the top of the section and adding 2–3 short animated conversation examples (typewriter effect) showing:

  • "Refactor this for performance" → Kitsune rewrites with explanation

6. Conversion & CTA

MEDIUM IMPACT
Add a persistent bottom CTA bar on scroll

When users scroll past the hero, they lose the download CTA. A sticky bottom bar (appears after 50% scroll) keeps the conversion path visible without being intrusive.

COPY
🍁 Momiji IDE — Free download · v1.2.0 · Windows / macOS / Linux   [Download Free ↓]
QUICK WIN
Add a "Pricing" link in the nav

The current nav has: Features · Kitsune AI · Download · GitHub. Adding "Pricing" (even if it's currently all free) sets the expectation that there will be paid tiers and makes the product feel more serious to investors and potential enterprise clients.
